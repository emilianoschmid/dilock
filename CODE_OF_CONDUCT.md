# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique technical ideas and evidence, not people's identity or worth. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Assume contributions can improve through review. Explain the safety or compatibility issue and suggest a concrete correction where possible. Do not pressure contributors to reveal credentials or personal details.

Maintainers may edit/remove abusive contributions or restrict participation proportionately. A private reporting route and responsible maintainer identities must be configured and documented before opening the project broadly; this blueprint does not invent a committee or email address.
